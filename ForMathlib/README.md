# ForMathlib

General results developed for the formalization of Roquette's *Analytic theory of elliptic
functions over local fields* that are not (yet) in Mathlib. The files only import Mathlib (never
`AnalyticTheory`), use Mathlib's naming conventions and namespaces, and are stated in the
generality we could reach, so they can be upstreamed independently. `AnalyticTheory` uses them
through thin wrappers in the book's notation.

| File | Content | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| `NumberTheory/LambertSeries.lean` | Lambert series `∑ F(m) qᵐ/(1−qᵐ) = ∑ (∑_{d∣n} F(d)) qⁿ` for any bounded `F` in a complete normed field (`hasSum_lambert`, `hasSum_lambert_pnat`). Mathlib only has `F(m) = mᵏ`. | `QSeries`, `buzzard` |
| `RingTheory/PowerSeries/TsumEval.lean` | Evaluation `R⟦X⟧ →+* K` at `‖q‖ < 1` along `φ : R →+* K` with `‖φ r‖ ≤ 1` (`PowerSeries.tsumEvalHom`), the bound `‖f(q) − a₀‖ ≤ ‖q‖/(1−‖q‖)`, and the identity principle `PowerSeries.eq_zero_of_tsumEval_eq_zero`. | `Sec3VII`, `Delta7`, `buzzard` |
| `Algebra/Polynomial/LaurentRoots.lean` | A Laurent polynomial with infinitely many unit zeros (after an injective `R →+* S`, `S` a domain) is zero. | `buzzard` |
| `Analysis/Normed/Field/SqrtOneAddFour.lean` | In a complete ultrametric field with `2 ≠ 0`, `1 + 4s` is a square for `‖s‖ < 1` (Newton iteration). | `Sec3VIII` |
| `Analysis/SpecificLimits/EulerProduct.lean` | Convergence and non-vanishing of `∏ (1 − q^{n+m} y)`, and Euler's identity `∏(1+qⁿ) ∏(1−q^{2k+1}) = 1`. | `Delta4` |
| `Topology/Algebra/InfiniteSum/NonarchimedeanProd.lean` | Double series in complete nonarchimedean groups (Fubini for families tending to `0`), domination criterion for summability in ultrametric groups, `s^{m+n} → 0` on `ℕ × ℕ`. | `QSeries` |
| `FieldTheory/ExtendRingEquiv.lean` | Ext lemmas for homomorphisms out of `F(s)`; `deg minpoly = [E : F]` for a generator; extension of an isomorphism `E ≃ E'` of subfields with `F = E(a) = E'(a)` to an automorphism of `F` fixing `a`. | `FqDescent`, `A1LemmaA` |
| `Analysis/SpecialFunctions/CircleCharacters.lean` | A character `χ : G →* Circle` of a finite group has cyclic image, generated by a value `exp(2πi/n)`; `exp(2πi/n)` has order `n`. | `Lemma2b` |
| `AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/IsomOfJTwist.lean` | Over an **arbitrary** field: the twist (Hasse) invariant `γ` of a Weierstraß curve, its invariance under change of variables, and the classification: for `j ≠ 0, 1728`, two elliptic curves are isomorphic over `K` iff they have the same `j` and equivalent `γ` (`WeierstrassCurve.exists_variableChange_iff_of_j_ne`). This complements Mathlib's `exists_variableChange_of_j_eq` (separably closed fields). | `A1Normal`, `A1Classify`, `A1LemmaA` |

## Further candidates (not yet extracted)

These parts of `AnalyticTheory` are also general mathematics missing from Mathlib, but are
currently phrased in terms of the project's objects and need a refactoring before they can be
moved:

* **Non-archimedean function theory on annuli** (`Gauss.lean`, `GaussNorm.lean`, `Dominance.lean`,
  `Identity.lean`, `Zeros.lean`, `WeierstrassPrep.lean`, `HolDiv.lean`, `FrechetLaurent.lean`,
  `InfProd.lean`): convergent Laurent series over a complete ultrametric field, multiplicativity of
  Gauss norms and additivity of extremal indices, the identity theorem, finiteness of zeros in
  closed annuli, Weierstraß preparation, the division theorem `w_α(A) ≥ w_α(B) ∀α ⇒ B ∣ A`, and
  infinite products. They are stated for functions on `Ω^×` (`holSubring K`); the natural Mathlib
  form is in terms of sequences `ℤ → K` with `‖aₙ‖ rⁿ → 0` for all `r > 0`.
* **Function fields of one variable** (`FunctionField.lean`, `EllipticBasics.lean`, `A1Normal.lean`,
  `A1Unique.lean`): prime divisors as valuation rings, Riemann–Roch spaces, `dim L(n𝔭) = n` for an
  elliptic field with a prime of degree one, and the Weierstraß model `F ≅ K(X)[Y]/(P)`. These rely
  on the project's definition of the genus via Riemann–Roch.
* **Identities of integer `q`-series** (`Delta7.lean`, `buzzard.lean`, `Sec3VII.lean`): Jacobi's
  `Δ = q ∏(1−qⁿ)²⁴` with `Δ` expressed through `σ₃`, `σ₅`, Buzzard's identity for the Tate curve
  in `ℚ(u)⟦q⟧`, and `j = 1/q + R(q)` with `R ∈ ℤ⟦q⟧`. The statements are Mathlib-ready, but their
  proofs currently go through the analytic theory above.
* **Galois-theoretic lemmas** (`Lemma2b.lean`, `Sec4.lean`): `N(x) = ∏ σʲ x` for a generator `σ`
  of a cyclic Galois group, and products over a cyclic group as products over powers of a
  generator.
